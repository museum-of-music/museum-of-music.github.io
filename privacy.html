Privacy

Your privacy at the museum

The short version: we only ask for your email address, we only use it to invite you to new exhibitions, and we never sell it or share it with anyone.

What we collect

The only personal information this site collects is your email address, and only if you choose to type it into the sign-up form on our home page. That form is provided by beehiiv, the service we use for our emails.

How we use it

We use your email address for one thing: to send you an occasional invitation when a new exhibition opens. That's it. No daily newsletters, no ads, and no emails about anything else.

Where it's kept

Your email address is stored by beehiiv, and beehiiv is also the service that sends our emails. You can read beehiiv's own privacy policy to see how they look after it.

We never sell or share it

We will never sell your email address, rent it, trade it, or share it with anyone else. It stays with us and with beehiiv, which only uses it to deliver our emails.

How to unsubscribe

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, and one click is all it takes. If you'd rather ask a person, just write to curator@museum-of-music.com and we'll take you off the list.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any tracking cookies of its own. The sign-up form is loaded from beehiiv, so beehiiv's own privacy policy covers anything that form does.

Questions

If you have any questions about your privacy, write to the curator at curator@museum-of-music.com. We're happy to help.
